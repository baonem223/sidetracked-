SIDETRACK SKATE SHOP

Digital Advertising Campaign

Supporting Local Skateboarding in Ames, Iowa

Campaign Overview

This digital advertising campaign promotes Sidetrack Skate Shop, a locally owned skate shop in Ames, Iowa. The campaign focuses on increasing awareness of the shop, attracting new customers, and strengthening its relationship with the local skateboarding community.

The advertisements emphasize Sidetrack's connection to skate culture and the importance of supporting independent skate shops. Through consistent branding, recognizable artwork, and clear calls to action, the campaign encourages potential customers to explore Sidetrack and learn more about its products and community involvement.

Four digital advertisement formats are used to demonstrate how the campaign can adapt across different website placements while maintaining a consistent visual identity. Each advertisement directs visitors to the same campaign landing page.

Campaign Goals

  • Increase Brand Awareness: Introduce Sidetrack Skate Shop to more potential customers in Ames and the surrounding communities.
  • Drive Website Traffic: Encourage users to click the advertisements and visit the campaign landing page.
  • Support Local Business: Highlight the value of shopping at an independently owned skate shop rather than relying solely on larger retailers.
  • Engage the Skate Community: Connect with skateboarders, students, and individuals interested in skate culture and streetwear.
  • Maintain Consistent Branding: Use coordinated visuals and messaging across all four advertisement sizes.

Target Audience & Strategy

The primary target audience consists of skateboarders, Iowa State University students, and young adults interested in skateboarding, streetwear, and local culture. Secondary audiences include beginners looking to purchase their first skateboard and community members interested in supporting local businesses.

The campaign uses a combination of brand recognition, community-focused messaging, and direct calls to action. The four ad dimensions allow the campaign to appear in banner, sidebar, and display placements across different websites.

Measuring Campaign Success

The campaign's effectiveness can be evaluated using impressions, click-through rates (CTR), landing page visits, and visitor engagement.

Tracking these metrics can help determine which advertisement sizes and messages generate the strongest audience response. This information can guide future improvements to Sidetrack's digital advertising strategy.
