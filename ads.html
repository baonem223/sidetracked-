THE AD CAMPAIGN

SIDETRACK SKATE SHOP • DISPLAY ADVERTISING

CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW

This digital advertising campaign promotes Sidetrack Skate Shop and its collection of skateboards, fingerboards, clothing, and original artwork.

The purpose of the campaign is to increase brand awareness, attract potential customers, and encourage visitors to explore the shop.

TARGET AUDIENCE

The primary target audience includes teenagers and young adults ages 16–34 who are interested in skateboarding, streetwear, alternative fashion, and creative culture.

Interest-based targeting would focus on skateboarding, skateboard equipment, street fashion, action sports, and original artwork. The campaign would primarily target potential customers in the United States.

CAMPAIGN GOALS

The main goals are to increase brand recognition, generate website traffic, encourage product discovery, and drive potential purchases.

CREATIVE HOOK

The campaign uses bright yellow backgrounds, bold pink typography, playful skateboard illustrations, and a prominent "Shop Now" call to action.

These creative elements are intended to capture attention and reflect the energetic, artistic, and independent nature of skateboarding culture.

BENCHMARK ESTIMATES
