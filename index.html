OUR SEMESTER PROJECT

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Throughout the semester, our group has evaluated SIDETRACK's online presence to identify opportunities to improve its website and digital marketing effectiveness. SIDETRACK primarily sells skateboards, stickers, and T-shirts, making its website an important way to showcase its products and connect with potential customers.

Our project examines the website's user interface, user experience, search engine optimization (SEO), and technical performance. We have explored how website design, navigation, written content, image descriptions, and other on-site elements influence how visitors understand the business and find its products.

Through our audit, we aim to identify areas for improvement and provide practical recommendations that support a clearer, more accessible, and more effective online shopping experience. This project allows us to apply concepts from web development and digital marketing to a real business website.
