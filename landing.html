SKATE LOCAL. STAY SIDETRACKED.

Skateboarding is more than just a sport. It's a community built around creativity, individuality, and self-expression. Sidetrack Skate Shop brings that culture together through skateboards, streetwear, and original artwork.

EXPLORE SIDETRACK

01 / SKATEBOARDS & GEAR

Whether you're building your first skateboard or upgrading your current setup, Sidetrack Skate Shop celebrates everything skateboarding. Explore the culture surrounding decks, trucks, wheels, and accessories designed for skateboarders of all experience levels.

02 / STREETWEAR & STYLE

Skateboarding and fashion go hand in hand. Discover the influence of skate culture on clothing, footwear, and everyday style. Sidetrack embraces individuality and creative expression both on and off the board.

03 / ART & COMMUNITY

Skateboarding is driven by the people behind it. From original artwork and creative collaborations to the local skateboarding scene, Sidetrack represents the connection between skateboarding, art, and community.

SUPPORT YOUR LOCAL SKATE SHOP.

Independent skate shops play an important role in keeping local skateboarding communities alive. Supporting Sidetrack means supporting the creativity, culture, and people that make skateboarding unique.
